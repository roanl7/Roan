## What Makes It Interesting

It's positioned as a targeted evening step rather than a full routine overhaul — something that can be added to what someone already does without disrupting it.

Anyone looking to add a dedicated serum step to their evening skincare routine, particularly those interested in the Pro-Collagen line specifically.

- Lightweight serum formula designed for evening use
- Fits easily into an existing skincare routine
- Part of ELEMIS's established Pro-Collagen range

For people already curious about the ELEMIS Pro-Collagen line, this serum is a reasonable, low-commitment way to try it as an added evening step.
