## Why People Are Talking About It

Shorter gift terms are often chosen for casual readers, students, or as a lighter-touch gift alongside something else.

It's enough time to get through a handful of titles and see whether the habit sticks, without locking the recipient into a longer plan.

## Who It's For

Anyone gifting Audible to someone new to audiobooks, or as a smaller, lower-commitment gift.

Exact terms, included credits, and redemption details are set by Amazon/Audible and can change — check the current listing before purchasing as a gift.

## Our Take

A sensible way to let someone try Audible properly without committing them to a longer membership on your behalf.

## Who Should Consider It

Anyone looking for a smaller, introductory gift for someone curious about audiobooks.
