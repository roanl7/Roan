Most desk setups don't need a full overhaul. They need two or three targeted changes that remove daily friction — and it's worth being specific about which ones actually matter.

## Start With the Chair

Before screens or accessories, the chair is doing the most work of anything on this list, simply because it's in contact with your body for hours at a time. A supportive, adjustable chair changes how the last two hours of a workday feel more than almost anything else.

## Then the Display

Screen real estate is the next lever. Whether that means a second monitor or a single wide display depends on preference, but the goal is the same: fewer minimized windows, less alt-tabbing, more of your work visible at once.

## Input Devices Matter More Than People Expect

A better mouse or keyboard sounds like a minor upgrade until you've used one for a week. Precision, comfort, and wireless freedom add up over a full day of use.

## What to Skip

Elaborate lighting rigs, matching cable management systems, and most "gaming" branded add-ons are aesthetic choices, not functional ones — nice if the budget allows, unnecessary if it doesn't.
